1. Information We Collect

TRaDeiTBe is a backtesting and forward-testing application designed to help users record, test, track, and analyze their own trading ideas and results.

Information entered into the application may include:

  • Trading test results
  • Trade outcomes such as WIN or LOSS
  • Points or values associated with trades
  • Dates and times entered by the user
  • Notes entered by the user
  • Backtesting or forward-testing records
  • Application settings and preferences

This information is used to provide the application's testing, tracking, calculation, statistics, chart, and history features.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Information entered into TRaDeiTBe may be stored locally on your device so that the application can remember your testing records and settings.

Local information may remain on your device until you delete it through the application's available controls, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or otherwise remove the stored information.

We do not claim ownership of the trading records or notes that you create within the application.

3. Personal Information

TRaDeiTBe does not require users to provide sensitive personal information in order to use its core backtesting and forward-testing functionality.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into trade notes or other free-text fields.

If you voluntarily enter personal information into the application, that information may be stored as part of the content you create.

4. Data Transmission and Sharing

The core functionality of TRaDeiTBe is designed to work with information entered by the user on the device.

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

We do not intentionally share users' locally stored trading records with third parties unless such sharing is required to provide a specific feature, required by law, or otherwise disclosed to users.

If future versions of TRaDeiTBe introduce services that transmit information to external servers or third-party service providers, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

5. Third-Party Services

TRaDeiTBe may use third-party services in certain versions of the application, such as services required for application distribution, crash reporting, analytics, advertising, or other technical functionality.

Where third-party services are used, those providers may process certain technical or device-related information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Relevant third-party data practices will be disclosed in the applicable version of this Privacy Policy and in Google Play's Data safety disclosures where required.

6. Advertising

If advertising is enabled in TRaDeiTBe, advertisements may be provided by third-party advertising providers.

Advertising providers may collect or process certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, or information about interactions with advertisements, depending on the services used and the user's device settings.

The availability and type of advertising may change between versions of the application.

7. Analytics and Crash Reporting

If analytics or crash-reporting services are enabled in a particular version of TRaDeiTBe, technical information may be processed to help us understand application performance, diagnose crashes, and improve reliability.

Such information may include the application version, device type, operating system version, crash information, and other technical diagnostic information, depending on the service used.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by TRaDeiTBe.

However, no electronic storage system or method of transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own devices and should use appropriate device security measures.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because TRaDeiTBe may store testing records locally on the user's device, users can manage or delete their records using the application's available deletion controls.

Users may also delete locally stored application information by clearing the application's data or uninstalling TRaDeiTBe, subject to the behavior of the user's device and operating system.

If a future version of TRaDeiTBe stores information on external servers, additional deletion procedures may be provided and this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Children's Privacy

TRaDeiTBe is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through the core functionality of the application.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

11. Financial Information and Trading Disclaimer

TRaDeiTBe is a testing, tracking, and educational tool.

The application does not execute trades, hold funds, provide brokerage services, or act as a financial institution.

TRaDeiTBe does not provide personalized financial, investment, or trading advice.

Any trading information, calculations, statistics, or results displayed by the application are provided for testing and informational purposes only.

Users are solely responsible for their own trading decisions and activities.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to TRaDeiTBe, applicable laws, third-party services, or our data practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
